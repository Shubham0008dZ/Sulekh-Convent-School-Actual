Our Curriculum

Academic Framework

A well-structured curriculum balancing academics, co-curricular, and life skills.

CBSE / UP Board Affiliated

Our school follows the CBSE/UP Board curriculum ensuring national standard education.

Nursery to Class XII

Complete schooling from nursery through senior secondary under one roof.

Medium of Instruction

English medium with Hindi as second language and Sanskrit/Urdu as optional.

Structure

Classes Offered

Pre-School (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Our pre-school program focuses on play-based learning to develop foundational skills in language, numbers, creativity, and social interaction. Caring teachers create a warm, stimulating environment for your child's first steps in education.

  • Activity-based learning
  • Rhymes, stories & art
  • Fine motor skill development
  • Social skills & confidence building

Primary School (Class I – V)

Primary classes build strong conceptual foundations. Students develop reading, writing, and arithmetic skills while exploring science and social studies through interactive lessons and projects.

  • Strong foundation in core subjects
  • Project-based learning
  • Regular assessments
  • Moral education integrated

Middle School (Class VI – VIII)

Middle school deepens subject knowledge and introduces students to critical thinking, research skills, and broader academic concepts. Regular lab work and project presentations prepare them for senior classes.

  • Subject-specialist teachers
  • Science practicals
  • Debate & public speaking
  • Sports & arts integration

Secondary (Class IX – X)

Class IX and X focus on board exam preparation with rigorous academic training. Regular mock tests, doubt-clearing sessions, and expert faculty guidance ensure excellent results in board examinations.

  • Board exam-focused preparation
  • Regular mock tests & revisions
  • Individual doubt-clearing sessions
  • Career counselling begins

We offer Science, Commerce, and Arts streams in Class XI–XII. Expert faculty, well-equipped labs, and comprehensive study material ensure students achieve their best in board exams and competitive entrance tests.

  • JEE/NEET/CA foundation guidance
  • Personality development programs
  • College application assistance

Beyond Academics

Co-Curricular Activities

We believe in all-round development. Our students excel both inside and outside the classroom.

Sports & Athletics

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, athletics, badminton — competitive training and inter-school tournaments.

Music & Dance

Vocal and instrumental music training along with classical and folk dance forms.

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, pottery, and handcraft activities developing creativity and fine motor skills.

Debate & Elocution

Public speaking, debate competitions, and elocution contests to build confidence and communication.

NCC / Scouts

National Cadet Corps and Scout activities for discipline, leadership, and patriotism.

Drama & Skits

Annual drama productions, skit competitions, and cultural programs on national events.
