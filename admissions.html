How to Apply

Admission Process

Simple, transparent, and parent-friendly admission procedure.

01

Collect Admission Form

Visit school office or download form from website. Form available on all working days between 9 AM – 2 PM.

02

Submit Application

Fill the form completely and submit with all required documents at the school office.

03

Interaction / Test

Student interaction session (for Nursery–Class 2) or written test (Class 3 onwards) as applicable.

04

Fee Payment & Enrollment

On selection, pay fees within the stipulated time and complete enrollment formalities.

Criteria

Eligibility Criteria

Nursery

Age: 3–4 years as on 1st April of admission year.

LKG / UKG

LKG: 4–5 years | UKG: 5–6 years as on 1st April.

Class I – V

Age appropriate as per class. TC and Report Card from previous school required.

Class VI – X

Promotion certificate from previous class. Entrance test may be conducted.

Class XI

Class X board result (minimum 50% marks). Stream allocation based on marks and preference.

Required

Documents Required

Birth Certificate (Original + Photocopy)
Aadhaar Card (Student + Parents)
Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school
Previous class Report Card / Marksheet
Passport size photographs (4 copies)
Residence Proof (Electricity bill / Voter ID)
Caste Certificate (if applicable)
Medical Fitness Certificate
