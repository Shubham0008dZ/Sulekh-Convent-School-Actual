Fee Structure

Academic Year 2025–26

Transparent and affordable fee structure for all classes.

Important Notes

  • Fee once paid is non-refundable except under special circumstances.
  • Late fee of ₹50 per day will be charged after due date.
  • Fee can be paid by cash, DD or online transfer at school office.
  • For fee concession, apply to the Principal with supporting documents.
  • Annual charges include: tuition, computer, library, sports, and miscellaneous.
