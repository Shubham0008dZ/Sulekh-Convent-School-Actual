Our Story

Sulekh Convent School – Overview

Sulekh Convent School, situated in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, stands as a beacon of quality education in the region. Established with a vision to provide world-class education to every child, our school has grown to become one of the most respected institutions in Bareilly.

Our school offers a nurturing environment where academic excellence meets holistic development. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, highly qualified faculty, and a comprehensive curriculum, we prepare our students not just for exams, but for life.
